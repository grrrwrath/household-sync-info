Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 November 2026.

Household Sync is a private household setup using rclone to synchronise selected document folders between household computers and Google Drive.

Data access and purpose

Google Drive authorisation permits rclone to access file contents, names, folder structure and file metadata, and to create, modify or delete files for two-way synchronisation. Although the authorisation scope is broad, configured sync jobs target selected folders.

Storage and sharing

Documents are stored in Google Drive and on the household computers configured for syncing. Recovery copies and operational logs may be retained for troubleshooting and restoration. Credentials are stored locally by rclone and must be protected.

Access by other household members is controlled through Google Drive sharing and local device permissions. Google processes cloud data under its own policies. This setup does not sell Google user data or use it for advertising or model training.

This website

This site contains informational pages only. It does not receive documents or OAuth credentials and includes no analytics or application cookies. GitHub Pages may process ordinary hosting request information under GitHub’s policies.

Control and removal

The account owner can stop syncing and revoke access through Google Account security settings. Revocation does not remove existing local, cloud or recovery copies; those must be managed separately. Household members should contact the account owner directly with questions or removal requests.
