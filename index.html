Household Sync

A private household document synchronisation setup using rclone to keep selected folders on household Macs in sync with Google Drive.

The Google OAuth application labelled Household Sync authorises rclone to list, read, create, update and delete files as required for two-way synchronisation. Sync jobs are configured to operate on selected household document folders.

This setup is for the account owner and their household. It is not a public service, and this website does not provide sign-in or access to documents.
